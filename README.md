## Featured projects

### 🛒 [E-commerce Sales & Customer Behaviour Analytics](https://github.com/Abhishek0419-cloud/Ecommerce-Project)
`MySQL` `Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn`

15 business questions on 99K marketplace orders across 6 relational tables, answered with joins, CTEs and window functions.

- Top 5 product categories drive **~40% of revenue**.
- Only **2.3%** of 96K customers reorder within 6 months of their first purchase (3.1% ever), pointing to a retention gap.
- Jan–Aug 2018 sales grew **137%** over 2017 (like-for-like months).
- Found and fixed a duplicate-load bug (6× inflated data) and a join fan-out that double-counted revenue.

### 📉 [Customer Churn Analysis](https://github.com/Abhishek0419-cloud/Customer-Churn-Analysis)
`Python` `Pandas` `Seaborn`

Exploratory analysis of telecom customer churn (26.5% overall).

- **Month-to-month contracts drive 89% of churn** (42.7% vs 2.8% on two-year plans); 55% of churners leave within 12 months.
- Customers with neither Online Security nor Tech Support churn at **49% vs 9%** for those with both.
- Electronic Check users churn at 45% vs 16% for auto-pay, but 78% of them are month-to-month. After controlling for contract, the gap narrows to 54% vs 33%.

## How I work

- **I validate before I report.** Row counts, totals and joins are checked against the source data, and results that look too good or too big get investigated first.
- **I state limits.** Churn findings are associations, not causes, and my write-ups say what each analysis cannot show.
- **I end with a recommendation**, not just a chart.
